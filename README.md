# 🧾 FMCG Analytics Project

## 📘 Overview
This project analyzes sales, revenue, and performance data from a **Fast-Moving Consumer Goods (FMCG)** dataset using **Microsoft Excel**.  
The goal is to uncover insights into **sales trends**, **product performance**, **regional contributions**, and **profitability**, helping business stakeholders make data-driven decisions.

---

## ⚙️ Tools Used
- **Microsoft Excel**
  - Data Cleaning  
  - Pivot Tables  
  - Charts & Dashboards  
  - Conditional Formatting  
  - Lookup & Aggregation Functions (`VLOOKUP`, `INDEX-MATCH`, `SUMIFS`, etc.)

---

## 🧩 Key Objectives
1. Analyze **total sales performance** across regions and time periods.  
2. Identify **top-performing products and categories**.  
3. Track **revenue trends** and **profit margins**.  
4. Measure **regional and outlet-wise contributions**.  
5. Build an **interactive dashboard** for management reporting.

---

## 📊 Project Workflow

### 1. Data Cleaning
- Removed duplicates and blank rows.  
- Standardized product names, dates, and region fields.  
- Handled missing and inconsistent entries.

### 2. Data Preparation
- Added calculated columns for:
  - **Profit**
  - **Profit Margin (%)**
  - **Sales Growth (%)**
- Segmented data by **Region**, **Category**, and **Channel**.

### 3. Analysis
- Created **Pivot Tables** for sales by region, category, and product.  
- Applied **Conditional Formatting** to highlight top/bottom performers.  
- Built **trend charts** to visualize monthly and quarterly performance.

### 4. Dashboard Creation
Designed a **dynamic Excel dashboard** featuring:
- **Slicers** for Product Category, Region, and Time Period  
- **KPIs** including:
  - Total Sales  
  - Total Profit  
  - Top 5 Products  
  - Sales by Region  
  - Monthly Sales Trend  

---

## 💡 Insights Gained
- Identified top-selling products and underperforming SKUs.  
- Found region-level demand variations affecting total revenue.  
- Observed steady sales growth in key categories (e.g., Beverages, Snacks).  
- Highlighted opportunities for cost optimization in low-margin segments.
